Qt for Python (PySide6) and Qt
==============================

JASE uses Qt for Python (PySide6) and the Qt libraries, which are licensed
under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 (LGPL-3.0).

JASE is distributed as a PyInstaller "onedir" folder: the Qt and PySide6
libraries are shipped as separate dynamic libraries (DLLs) inside the folder,
so they can be replaced by a compatible version by the user.
